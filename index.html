01About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics, Mahindra University, Hyderabad. Before that, I was a Post-Doctoral Fellow at the School of Mathematics, IISER Thiruvananthapuram, and at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Centre for Applicable Mathematics (TIFR-CAM), Bangalore. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from the Department of Mathematics, Indian Institute of Technology Bombay.

My research is on the development of numerical schemes for hyperbolic conservation laws. In particular, I am interested in Weighted Essentially Non-Oscillatory (WENO), Discontinuous Galerkin (DG) and hybrid methods. In recent work, we developed constraint-preserving Discontinuous Galerkin methods for the ideal magnetohydrodynamics equations.

06Project

Research project

Weighted Essentially Non-Oscillatory (WENO) Reconstruction

WENO schemes are high-order accurate spatial reconstruction techniques widely used to simulate compressible flows, since they resolve small-scale structures at high resolution and capture shocks without spurious oscillations. Introduced by Liu et al. in the finite-volume framework and extended to finite differences by Jiang and Shu, they have developed rapidly over the last two decades. We recently developed new fifth- and seventh-order WENO schemes of adaptive order for 2D problems; this project aims to build an efficient adaptive-order WENO scheme for 3D model problems.

Related publications: [JCP 2018], [Computers & Fluids 2019], [J. Sci. Comput. 2020], [Wave Motion 2020], [IJNMF 2021] — see Publications.
